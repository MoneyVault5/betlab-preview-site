Checking feeds…

DraftKings and Novig have no public feed this page may use, so they always show UNAVAILABLE rather than odds. Kalshi and Polymarket expose public market endpoints; ESPN exposes a public scoreboard. If a fetch is blocked (network/CORS), that feed shows UNAVAILABLE — it is never filled in.

Pregame Scout — NFL / NBA

Games come from the public ESPN scoreboard. Matchup notes below are computed from those records only (records, venue, status). Injury and defense-depth detail requires a licensed feed and is shown as unavailable where it cannot be fetched — it is not guessed.

Live Scout

Live scores/clocks come from the public ESPN scoreboard at fetch time; every refresh is timestamped. In-game player-stat depth and win-probability estimates require a licensed feed and are marked unavailable when not fetched — no estimate is invented.

Market Comparison

Quotes are fetched live from public endpoints. American odds and implied probability are computed from the quote (implied = 100/(odds+100) for favorites, 100/(100+|odds|) for underdogs — for 0–1 prices, implied = price). DraftKings / Novig: UNAVAILABLE — no public feed.

Edge/EV use YOUR probability only. Blank = UNCALIBRATED.

AI Performance Tracker

Append-only log, stored in this browser only (key ai_scout_tracker_v1). A logged prediction is never edited; only its outcome is later marked Won / Lost / Void. Win rate, ROI, and calibration are computed from logged entries only — with zero settled entries they read 0 / n/a, never a borrowed rate. This log prevents look-ahead bias: a prediction's timestamp is fixed at log time and outcomes can only be marked after it.
